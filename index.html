How to use
  • Fill in figures: press Import quote, or paste a screenshot with Ctrl+V. Works with screenshots, PDFs and Excel sheets.
  • Change anything: click the text and type.
  • Quick answers: click a cell and pick Yes, No or NA.
  • Add: + Insurer, + Row, + Section, or + beside a section to add a row there.
  • Remove: click × next to a row, section or insurer.
  • Send: press Copy for Outlook, then Ctrl+V in your email.
